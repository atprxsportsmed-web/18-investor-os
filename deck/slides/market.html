Research notes (November 2026, from public listings, verify before use): Hamilton County had about 386,000 residents in 2024 (Census). The National Golf Foundation reports about 29 million on-course golfers in the US in 2025, roughly 8 to 9 percent of Americans, which applied to the county gives about 30,000 on-course golfers; the 20-minute radius and the 35 to 55 share are not yet separated out, and the NGF age data is behind a paywall. Competition: The Back Nine and Albatross Golf Sim & Bar are bar-style venues. Choo Choo Golf Academy and Range (Mackey Ave) is the closest competitor. Its 12 bays are outdoor range spots; it has two indoor bays, only one bookable, at $50 an hour. It uses a force plate to measure swing sequencing, not athleticism, and does not collect member data or test the body. It does not list strength training, recovery or clinical care. The public listing overstated its indoor capacity, so confirm details directly before quoting them. Black Creek, Chattanooga Golf and Country Club and Council Fire are private clubs with ranges and lessons; they are not treated as competitors but as a source of members and partners. The founder reports the only direct competitors are Back Nine, Albatross and Choo Choo. The $229 simulator membership is 24/7 access to simulators with about 2 hours a day bookable, and nothing else in the facility. One TPI-specialized PT in Chattanooga sells by session or package only; do not name him on a slide unless he agrees.
