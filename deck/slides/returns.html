These are cash-only returns: no sale value and no returned capital are counted, so they are deliberately conservative. At 5% monthly churn and dues-only revenue the Base case does not repay within five years. At 3% a month it does, in Year 5, so churn is the lever to discuss. The owner forgoing distributions until payback is the main strength of the structure.
