Why I built it

At 13, I was a skinny kid hitting hundreds of golf balls a day. When my back started hurting, I was told to stretch it. I made it to scratch, and then pain and life ended the run.

As a golfer, I wanted everything I needed in one place: someone who understood the swing, the body, the training and the injuries, and who made them work together. That place didn’t exist.

So I decided to build it, and to take it further than anything I had access to: every member tested, every plan connected across a team of specialists, and every result tracked.

Jeremy Herum, MS, LAT, ATC, CSCS · Founder
